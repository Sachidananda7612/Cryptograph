ACADEMIC BIBLIOGRAPHY • RECOMMENDED TEXTBOOKS

Academic References & Textbooks

Authoritative academic textbooks referenced for the mathematical foundations, key generation protocol, and cryptographic verification of the RSA Asymmetric Cryptosystem.

01
STANDARD TEXTBOOK Cryptography and Network Security: Principles and Practice
Author: William Stallings
Pearson Education, 7th & 8th Editions. Comprehensive reference for undergraduate and graduate computer science curricula.
Chapters on Prime Numbers and Modular Arithmetic (Chapter 2 & 4) and Public-Key Cryptography & RSA (Chapter 9). Detailed analysis of Euler's totient function, computational complexity of integer factorization, and RSA key distribution protocols.
02
ACADEMIC TEXTBOOK Introduction to Modern Cryptography
Authors: Jonathan Katz and Yehuda Lindell
CRC Press / Chapman & Hall, 3rd Edition (2020).
Rigorous mathematical treatment of public-key cryptography, trapdoor permutations, the factoring and RSA assumptions, and formal security reductions against passive and active adversaries.
03
THEORY & PRACTICE Cryptography: Theory and Practice
Authors: Douglas R. Stinson and Maura B. Paterson
CRC Press, 4th Edition (2018).
In-depth computational algorithms including the Extended Euclidean Algorithm for modular inverse, fast square-and-multiply exponentiation, and Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT) optimizations.
04
APPLIED TEXTBOOK Cryptography and Network Security
Authors: Behrouz A. Forouzan and Debdeep Mukhopadhyay
McGraw-Hill Education, 3rd Edition (2015).
Standard university textbook providing step-by-step numerical examples of RSA encryption, modular exponentiation, prime candidate selection, and asymmetric key exchange architectures.
05
OPEN ACCESS HANDBOOK Handbook of Applied Cryptography
Authors: Alfred J. Menezes, Paul C. van Oorschot, and Scott A. Vanstone
CRC Press (1996) — Available Freely Online via University of Waterloo.
The canonical reference handbook detailing exact algorithms for integer factorization, primality testing, public-key infrastructure, and asymmetric cipher security parameters.
