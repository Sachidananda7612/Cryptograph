LABORATORY EXPERIMENT PROTOCOL

Step-by-Step Procedure

Step 1: Choose Two Prime Numbers (p, q)

Navigate to the Simulation page. Enter two distinct prime numbers p and q (e.g., p = 11, q = 13 or p = 61, q = 53). The system will instantly check primality and distinctness.

Step 2: Calculate Modulus (n) and Euler's Totient φ(n)

Observe the computed values:
n = p × q   |   φ(n) = (p - 1)(q - 1)

Step 3: Choose Public Exponent (e)

Input your chosen public exponent e such that 1 < e < φ(n) and gcd(e, φ(n)) = 1. You can also pick from the dynamically suggested coprime chips.

Step 4: Compute Private Exponent (d)

The simulator executes the Extended Euclidean Algorithm to calculate d = e⁻¹ mod φ(n) such that e · d ≡ 1 mod φ(n). Key pairs (e, n) and (d, n) are generated.

Step 5: Enter Plaintext Key Number (M)

In the Workbench, enter a numeric plaintext key M ensuring 0 ≤ M < n (e.g., M = 4 or M = 65).

Step 6: Perform Encryption and Decryption

Click "1. Encrypt Key (M)" to compute Ciphertext: C = M^e mod n.
Then click "2. Decrypt Cipher (C)" to recover: M' = C^d mod n.
Verify that M' = M (100% verification match).
