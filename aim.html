IMPLEMENT ASYMMETRIC KEY DISTRIBUTION WITH RSA

Aim of the Experiment

To study and implement asymmetric key distribution using the RSA (Rivest–Shamir–Adleman) cryptosystem. This includes generating public-private key pairs (e, n) and (d, n), distributing the public key over an insecure channel, performing asymmetric encryption (c = m^e mod n) and decryption (m = c^d mod n), and understanding the computational hardness of integer factorization behind its security.
